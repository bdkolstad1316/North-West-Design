One counter. One website.
One system.

The short version: NWD's payment setup has been moved three times in four months — off CardPointe, onto Echelon's HYFIN page, then onto iPOSpays, the last time without notice. The online bill-pay link on nwtees.com simply died until someone caught it, and every fix runs through a support case and somebody else's schedule. That isn't bad luck — it's the structure: the card reader and the online payments are separate products stitched together by a reseller chain, and when any layer changes, the shop is the last to know.

A modern provider replaces that chain with one account that runs both sides of the business — the terminal at the counter, plus invoices, payment links, and the bill-pay page online — in one dashboard NWD controls directly.

The honest trade-off — and the alternative

Square's flat rate may run slightly above a negotiated merchant-account rate on some cards. What it buys is no middlemen, no surprise migrations, and software the shop runs itself. If shaving fees matters more than polish, Helcim ($0/month, interchange-plus — roughly 0.4% + 8¢ over cost in person) is the budget pick: same one-account model, fewer hardware options, two-day deposits.

What switching takes

Sign-up takes about 15 minutes, the terminal ships in days, and the website update is a same-day change. Run Square alongside the current setup until the first deposits clear, and check the Echelon paperwork for termination terms before closing the old account. Nothing about this switch takes the shop offline.
